About Us

Built to open doors into STEM.

GLITCH is a youth-led global initiative created to help girls explore, learn, and lead in technology, engineering, and innovation.

Who We Are

A growing community with a global mission.

Founded in 2026, GLITCH began with one simple belief: every girl deserves the opportunity to explore, create, and lead in technology. We believe talent is everywhere, but opportunities are not.

Our work centers on reducing barriers, opening access, and helping girls uncover what they are capable of through mentorship, workshops, projects, and peer support.

Our Story

From a vision to a shared movement.

GLITCH was founded in 2026 by Nnodiogu Jessica. What started as one person’s vision has grown into a shared mission supported by passionate volunteers, students, and leaders who want to make technology more accessible and inclusive.

Together, we are building a space where girls can learn, collaborate, innovate, and shape the future through technology.
