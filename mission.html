Our Mission

Empower girls with knowledge, confidence, and opportunity.

We want every girl to have the tools, support, and community needed to thrive in technology, computing, engineering, and innovation.

Mission

To empower through access and belonging.

We create opportunities for girls to build technical skills, feel confident, and discover paths into technology careers.

Vision

A world of girl leaders in tech.

We imagine a future where every girl can become a confident leader and innovator in technology and engineering.

Why We Started

To close the opportunity gap.

Girls have curiosity and potential, but often lack access to mentors, quality programs, and supportive communities. GLITCH exists to change that.
